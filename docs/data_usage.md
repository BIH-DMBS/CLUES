#	Usage policies


When utilizing data from the CLUES, it is imperative that users adhere to the terms of use associated with the various primary data sources. Each dataset within the CLUES is governed by specific usage policies, and compliance with these terms ensures the ethical and legal use of the data. Users are encouraged to review and understand the terms of use for each primary data source before downloading and employing the data in their research.


When utilizing data from CLUES, it is essential that users adhere to the terms of use specified by each primary data source. Every dataset integrated into the CLUES workflow is subject to its own licensing conditions and usage restrictions, which may vary depending on the data provider. Compliance with these terms is not only a legal requirement but also a matter of research integrity and responsible data stewardship.

Prior to downloading or employing any dataset, users are strongly encouraged to consult the associated usage policies to ensure proper citation, redistribution rights, and limitations on commercial or derivative use. Failure to comply with these conditions could result in legal or ethical ramifications, particularly when publishing or sharing derivative results.
