## Summer School 2025

The summer school took place at Campus Timone, Faculté des Sciences Médicales et Paramédicales d’Aix-Marseille Université, Aix-Marseille University, France, from 8–9 October 2025.

The Summer School enabled participants to apply cutting-edge research to investigate the relationship between environment, brain, and mental health, ranging from geospatial data, digital mental health, virtual brain modelling, biostatistical methods, Earth observation, and Virtual Reality to large-language models.

The Summer School on environmental and computational neuroscience, was organised by the the Horizon Europe Project ‘environMENTAL’, Institut de Neurosciences des Systèmes at Aix-Marseille University, the German Centre for Mental Health (DZPG), and the Nature ‘Earth Brain Health Commission.’

The clues framework was presented in a lecture at this event. Therefore notebooks had been prepared to give a example how to use CLUES. There are two notebooks: marseille_create_test_data.ipynb to create a dummy cohort and marseille_summerschool_2025.ipynb that makes use of the dummy cohort to give an overview on data aggration, linking, neighborhood and analysis. 

To run the notbooks the paths that link to geospatial data need to be set in alignment with your own infrastructure.

 
## Summer School on Environmental and Computational Neuroscience

### Campus Timone, Aix-Marseille University, France
### 8–9 October 2025

Our Summer School brought together a vibrant community of early-career researchers, data scientists, and neuroscientists to explore one of the most exciting frontiers in modern science: how the environments we live in shape the brain and influence mental health.

Hosted at the Campus Timone of the Faculté des Sciences Médicales et Paramédicales d’Aix-Marseille Université, the two-day program combined hands-on training, vivid scientific exchange, and a shared vision of advancing computational tools for mental health innovation.

**A Programme Bridging Worlds**

Participants engaged with cutting-edge methods spanning:

- Geospatial analytics and environmental exposure modelling

- Digital mental health and mobile sensing

- Virtual brain modelling and network neuroscience

- Biostatistics and data harmonisation

- Earth observation technologies

- Virtual Reality–based research

- Artificial intelligence and large-language models

These complementary fields together form the backbone of environmental neuroscience, enabling new insights into how cities, green spaces, pollution, and social environments interact with neural systems across the lifespan.

**Organised by Leading International Partners**

The event was made possible through collaboration among:

- The Horizon Europe project environMENTAL

- Institut de Neurosciences des Systèmes at Aix-Marseille University

- German Centre for Mental Health (DZPG)

- Nature Earth Brain Health Commission

Their combined expertise created a unique training environment at the intersection of planetary health and brain research.

**Introducing the CLUES Framework**

A key highlight was the introduction of CLUES, an open-source framework designed to support:

- data aggregation

- spatiotemporal linkage

- neighbourhood modelling

- scalable computational analysis

To showcase its capabilities, two interactive Jupyter notebooks were prepared for the Summer School:

- marseille_create_test_data.ipynb
Builds a dummy cohort for demonstration purposes

- marseille_summerschool_2025.ipynb
Provides practical exercises in aggregation, linking, neighbourhood analytics, and exploratory modelling

Participants could run the examples locally after configuring the geospatial data paths to match their own computing infrastructure. The shapefile used for createing the loactions of the dummy cohort are in the shape_borders folder.
